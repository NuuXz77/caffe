Our Story

More than coffee —
a community rooted
in good taste.

Grounded was born in 2018 from a simple obsession: what would happen if a small café in Bandung took specialty coffee as seriously as the best roasters in Tokyo or Melbourne?

We built relationships with farmers across Ethiopia, Flores, and Toraja — paying fair prices and visiting farms each harvest. Every bag we source has a story. Every cup we pour carries that story forward.
